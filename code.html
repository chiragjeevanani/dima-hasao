1. I LOVE DIMA HASAO

JATINGA VIEW POINT
& SHIVRAI TEMPLE

Jatinga, Dima Hasao

Famous for the mysterious bird phenomenon during monsoon evenings. The viewpoint offers breathtaking sunsets and panoramic views. The nearby Shivrai Temple is a peaceful spiritual place.

Scenic View Spiritual Place Sunset Point

2. HAFLONG TOWN

Haflong, Dima Hasao

The administrative headquarters of Dima Hasao. A beautiful hill town with pleasant climate, rich culture, local markets, and warm hospitality. Explore the local life, churches, temples and scenic landscapes.

Local Markets Cultural Sites Scenic Beauty

3. SILAIKUL PEAK

Silaikul, Dima Hasao

The highest peak in Dima Hasao offering stunning panoramic views of the entire hill district. Ideal for trekking, camping and nature lovers.

Trekking Camping Panoramic View

1. I LOVE DIMA HASAO

About I Love Dima Hasao

The "I Love Dima Hasao" viewpoint is one of the most iconic and Instagrammable spots in the district. It offers a breathtaking panoramic view of the surrounding hills, lush valleys and the beautiful town of Haflong.

It is a symbol of pride and love for the people of Dima Hasao.

Dima Hasao, Assam
